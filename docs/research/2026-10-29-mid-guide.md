The mid page reaches its build guide
====================================

`pultec-mid` was approved on 2026-10-29 with *"looks good. approved"* and its
page is now in `drafted/pultec/pultec-mid/`, declared by the board, exported to a
netlist, guarded by a test and printed in the build packet. It is the fifth and
last Pultec section board, and the pattern it follows is the one
`2026-10-29-guides-with-approved-schematics.md` established for the other four.
**Nothing here is a new design. Two things in that pattern did not transfer, and
both are below.**

Reproduce with:

```
make -C boards/pultec-mid check        the guard the declaration turns on
make -C boards/pultec-mid guide        the packet, schematic page and all
```

---

## 1. The hashes, before the copy and after it

**The page was verified against `docs/schematic/verdicts.md` before anything was
copied and again after.** Not one byte changed; `cmp` is clean against the
source.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| approved page, where it was | `docs/research/2026-10-29-mid-full-run/mid-hybrid.kicad_sch` |
| sha256 before the copy | `1f8de32150f322f8f040b2cef1d857df7b16e884bff409a0debd5ec3a7344ff8` |
| sha256 after the copy, after the SVG export, after the netlist export, after `make guide`, and after the scan below | **identical, every time** |

**The research directory was not moved or deleted.** It is the execution record;
the copy under `drafted/` is what a build guide points at.

**The hash is checked on every run rather than being a figure in this file.**
`tests/circuits/pultec-mid.test.ts` re-computes it and compares it with the
digest parsed out of `drafted/pultec/pultec-mid/RECORD.md`, so an edit to the
approved page fails instead of passing quietly. The digest is parsed out of the
record rather than written into the test, for the reason the shared body states:
a hash copied into a test file is a second place for one fact to be wrong.

### The render digest

`kicad-cli sch export svg` stamps one `<title>SVG Image created as … date …</title>`
line, so the raw file digest moves on every export whether or not the drawing
did. `RECORD.md` records sha256 of the SVG with that one line dropped -
`ccf3bbca81f169e2da8351fa4f382684b8f9a2680660ca791006c1bc12f5fa8b` - and it
**reproduced exactly on a re-export** on this machine with KiCad 10.0.5.
Cross-machine and cross-version stability is **unmeasured**, which is why
`verdicts.md` and `RECORD.md` both call that digest evidence and not a binding.

*(An incidental observation: the two exports fell in the same clock second, so
the whole-file digests matched too. That is luck, not stability, and it is
exactly why the volatile line is dropped rather than trusted.)*

## 2. What did NOT transfer from the other four boards

### (a) THE PAGE IS A3. The other four are A4.

`(paper "A3")` in the sheet, `1190.52 x 841.896 pts` out of `pdfinfo`. The
earlier run's figure - *"841.896 x 595.296 pt, A4 landscape"* - is a property of
those four pages and **not of the pattern**, and reading it as the pattern is
what would have produced a wrong measurement here. Both are landscape and both
are `Page rot: 0`.

**This broke the earlier run's own control.** That control compared one board's
packet page against another's and reported ~141,000 differing pixels. Handed
`pultec-mid` (3307 x 2339 px at 200 dpi) and `pultec-hi-cut` (2339 x 1654), the
comparator cannot compare them pixel for pixel at all. See §4 for what replaced
it - and note that **the refusal is itself the safer behaviour**: a comparator
that silently resized would have reported a number.

### (b) THE SHARED TEST BODY HARD-CODED ONE BOARD'S SELECTOR SYMBOL, AND THIS PAGE REFUTED IT

`tests/circuits/drafted-sheet.ts` asserted
`expect(problems.pinIdentity).toEqual([SELECTOR_PIN_DISAGREEMENT])` for every
board, on the stated reasoning that the mount lug is a property of the stock
symbol rather than of any circuit. **The reasoning is right and the conclusion
was too wide**: the symbol is not universal.

- the four six-way boards carry `Switch:SW_Rotary_1x6_MP`, whose mechanical mount
  lug `MP` is on no net in the model
  (`circuits/pultec/schematic/section-board.ts:162`, `docs/known-gaps.md` **G282**)
- `pultec-mid` carries `Switch:SW_Rotary_1x12`, which has **no `_MP` variant and
  therefore no lug**, and whose unused twelfth contact is declared not-connected
  in the model through `ComponentOverride.ncPins` - it shows up in the export as
  the one-member net `unconnected-(SW1-Pad12)` and `roundTrip` has nothing to say
  about it

So this page's `pinIdentity` is **empty**. `pinDisagreements` moved onto
`DraftedPageUnderTest` beside `valueDisagreements`, in the same form and for the
same reason, and the four existing files now pass `[SELECTOR_PIN_DISAGREEMENT]`
explicitly. **The empty list is asserted as a claim, not omitted** - this page's
pin numbering agrees with the model exactly, and a lug appearing on it later must
fail.

The old comment said *"the two disagreements EVERY drafted Pultec page carries"*.
It is corrected in place with the refutation shown, rather than widened: a shared
body that hard-codes one board's symbol choice is a check that passes for the
wrong reason the first time a page is drawn with a different symbol.

### (c) This board has two switches, so it carries two value disagreements

`SW1` holds `SW_Rotary_1x12` and the mode switch `SW2` holds `SW_SPDT` in their
`Value` fields, where the model holds `11-way selector` and `boost/off/cut`.
Same class as the other four carry for `SW1` alone; neither reuses
`SELECTOR_VALUE_DISAGREEMENT`, whose text names the six-way symbol.

## 3. The netlist, and what the declaration turns on

`sch` and `netlist` are declared together, because `tools/perfboard/declaration.ts:129`
accepts no other arrangement - *"half of it means nothing"*. The export went to
`tests/fixtures/pultec-mid.net`, where the other declared boards keep theirs, and
was produced **through `tools/perfboard/netlist-sync.ts`** rather than by hand: a
raw `kicad-cli sch export netlist` stamps the design section with a
machine-specific absolute path, which is what that module exists to normalise.
The committed fixture's first `(source ...)` reads
`drafted/pultec/pultec-mid/pultec-mid.generated-do-not-edit.kicad_sch`, and the
test asserts that - so a fixture exported from some other file fails.

Declaring the pair turns on `make/board.mk`'s `netlist-agrees`, which re-exports
from the sheet on **every run** and reconciles by content, never by timestamp,
and which **fails naming the file** when `tests/circuits/<board>.test.ts` does not
exist.

### Read back with the repository's own `importNetlist`

**Never a hand-written parser.** The earlier guide run's hand-rolled regex merged
all 15 hi-cut nets into one 51-member net while printing a clean figure, and it
was caught only by comparing against another run's count.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| components | **39** |
| nets | **29** |
| against the net sets `docs/research/2026-10-29-mid-full-run/verify-after.txt` recorded on the page when it was shown to him | **identical** - 29 names, no name on either side alone, and no name whose membership differs |

Not the empty `<components/>`/`<nets/>` a paren-broken sheet can produce, and the
comparison parses 29 nets out of the recorded file or throws, so it cannot pass
having read nothing.

### What `roundTrip` reports

`lib/schgen/gates/round-trip.ts` over the board's IR plus its designator
annotation, built with no layout and no render. **Not `expectSameCircuit`**,
which compares against the VeroRoute lowering - off-board footprints as `PADS<n>`,
pins numbered by `PAD_ORDER` index - and would report a disagreement on every
off-board part about a vocabulary neither side is wrong in.

| comparison | result |
| --- | --- |
| `componentIdentity` | **0** |
| `netMembership` | **0** |
| `pinIdentity` | **0** - see §2(b) |
| `values` | **2** - `SW1` and `SW2`, see §2(c) |
| `footprints` | **31**, every one reading `footprint "<none>"` |
| `footprintsUndeclared` | **8** - the five coil sections, the pot and both switches |

No part on the page carries a `Footprint` field at all; copperhead and akcli
write none. The test asserts that every footprint problem reads `"<none>"`, so a
problem naming a real footprint - the page and the model disagreeing about which
part is fitted - would fail, and it asserts the list is non-empty so the loop
cannot pass having examined nothing.

### Every reading has a positive control

- **the sheet digest** - the same bytes plus one more hash to a different value
- **`netMembership: []`** - moving one pin between the export's two largest nets
  reports **4 problems**. An empty problem list is the same shape whether the
  comparison agreed or never looked.
- **the footprint loop** - asserted non-empty
- **the parenthesis-depth and control-byte scan** - final depth **0**, lowest
  reached **0**, **0** C0 bytes outside tab/CR/LF, against **3496** LF bytes in
  the same pass. The control: the same scan over a copy with the final closing
  paren removed reads **depth 1**. On that break `kicad-cli sch export netlist`
  exited **3** and wrote no export - it did not exit 0 on this particular break,
  though the warning stands for other modes, which is why the components-and-nets
  check exists anyway. The perturbed copy was written to scratch; **the approved
  file was never touched**, and its hash is unchanged after the scan.
- **control bytes were built with `chr()`**, never typed as escapes into a tool
  parameter, and the file was checked by **counting bytes**. CLAUDE.md records
  four incidents from doing it the other way, one of them in the commit that
  added the rule.

## 4. What the guide's schematic page actually shows

`make -C boards/pultec-mid guide` writes `schematic.pdf` into the packet. **It is
a separate file, not a page inside `guide.pdf`** - the same split the earlier run
reported, and §5 below carries it forward.

| | measured |
| --- | --- |
| pages | **1** |
| page size | **1190.52 x 841.896 pt, A3 landscape** |
| rotation | **`Page rot: 0`** - upright, not rotated |
| differing pixels vs a direct `kicad-cli sch export pdf` of the drafted sheet, at 200 dpi | **0** of 7,735,073 |
| CONTROL 1 - the same comparator over the copperhead baseline draft of the **same board on the same A3 paper** | **188,145 differing pixels** |
| CONTROL 2 - the same comparator over another board's approved page | **refuses**: sizes differ, A3 vs A4 |
| positive control that the comparator saw anything | **140,194 dark pixels** on the packet page, grey < 128 |

So the packet's schematic page is pixel-identical to the approved sheet, and the
comparator that says so is one that moves.

**AND THE FIRST MEASUREMENT OF THAT WAS WRONG, recorded because it is the shape
of the failure this repository keeps hitting.** The first reference export was a
plain `kicad-cli sch export pdf`, and the comparison read **7,734,369 differing
pixels of 7,735,073** - all but 704 of them. Not a difference in the drawing: the
guide passes **`--black-and-white`** (`tools/guide/packet.ts:155`) and a plain
export plots KiCad's theme background, grey 244, where the packet's page is white
255. Binarising at grey < 128 had already cut the disagreement to 12,434 and at
grey < 200 to 1,784, which is what said the drawing was not the problem. **The
reference has to be produced with the flags the thing under test uses**, and a
comparator handed the wrong reference reports a confident number.

### Looked at, not only measured

Rendered at 150 dpi and read. Across the top: **Junction** with J1 and J2 (both
`Conn_01x05`), then **Hi boost**, **Hi cut**, **Lo cut** and **Lo boost** as
stand-in groups. Below them the **Mid** group spans the sheet width: `SW1` at its
left with contacts 1-12 and the common on 13, eleven selector runs going right to
seventeen capacitors `C1`-`C17`, five coil sections `L1`-`L5` on the
`coil_return` rail above them with the tap buses `tap_2h`, `tap_1h`, `tap_0r45h`,
`tap_0r22h`, `tap_0r1h` beneath each, and at the right `SW2` (`boost/off/cut`)
with `R1`, `R2`, `R3` and a ground. `RV1` sits left of the selector on
`sel_common`. Every group rectangle, every value and every label is inside the
sheet frame. The lower third of the page, below the Mid rectangle, is empty.

The title block prints, inside its box with nothing cut off at either end:

```
Sheet: /
File: pultec-mid.generated-do-not-edit.kicad_sch
Title: pultec-mid
Size: A3    Date: 2026-10-29                      Rev: A
KiCad E.D.A. 10.0.5                               Id: 1/1
```

**Nothing is clipped, nothing is rotated, nothing is scaled down, and the
schematic did not displace a layout image** - `guide.html` still carries four
inline `<svg>` elements and `guide.pdf`'s page 2 is still
*"Component side - designators"*, checked by rendering it. `guide.pdf` is
**12 pages of US letter**; the withheld notice is gone from the HTML entirely
(zero occurrences of *"withheld"*).

**This writer is not a qualified reviewer of a drawing and nothing above is a
judgement of one.** `EVALUATING-DRAWINGS.md` binds; these are observations and
measurements about what is on the page and where.

## 5. Where the existing tooling was insufficient

Reported rather than fixed, and none of it blocked the work. The first two are
the earlier run's findings, confirmed to carry over to this board unchanged.

1. **The guide's `Schematic` header row names the `.kicad_sch`, and somebody at a
   bench cannot open one.** Page 1 of `guide.pdf` reads
   `Schematic   drafted/pultec/pultec-mid/pultec-mid.generated-do-not-edit.kicad_sch`
   while the printable page is `schematic.pdf` in the same directory, unnamed.
   `schematicRow` in `tools/guide/schematic-notice.ts` prints `schematic.path`,
   which for `kind: "generated"` would be the PDF. **The two branches point a
   reader at two different kinds of thing for the same reason.**
2. **The schematic is a separate PDF, not a page of `guide.pdf`**, and nothing in
   the packet says the stack is two documents.
3. **§3's title-block notice is still carried by the FILE NAME and cannot be
   carried any other way.** The sheet's `title_block` holds only `title`, `date`
   and `rev`; writing a `comment` field would change the bytes and un-approve the
   page. See `drafted/README.md`.
4. **§3 asks for an SVG render git can diff, and KiCad stamps a timestamp into
   every one.** The digest in `RECORD.md` is of the file minus that line, and
   **nothing in the repository computes it**, so that comparison is still a script
   in a research directory rather than a check that re-runs.
5. **`drafted/` still has no tool answering "which pages are staged and do they
   still hash to their records".** The per-board tests answer it one board at a
   time through `make check`. §3's recutils register is still not built.
6. **`resolveSchematic`'s withholding gate now has no board exercising it.** All
   five Pultec section boards declare a page, so the notice branch is covered only
   by `tools/guide/schematic-notice.ts`'s own tests. Worth saying plainly: the
   gate was last demonstrated live by `pultec-mid`, and that demonstration is
   over. **Nothing was loosened to get here.**

## 7. What was not done

- **`docs/schematic/verdicts.md` was not edited.** No entry added or altered; the
  verdict was already recorded.
- **No rule, check, comment or doc clause says that warm words amount to
  approval.** The owner's parenthesis - *"do not generalize this into a dumb
  rule"* - binds, and `EVALUATING-DRAWINGS.md` §5 still reads that approval is
  state the reviewer writes, never derived. `RECORD.md` quotes both the verdict
  and the scoped ruling and says so explicitly.
- **The page was not edited.** Not one byte changed, before or after; the
  perturbed copy for the paren control was written to scratch.
- **`circuits/pultec/electrical/` was not touched.**
- **`circuits/pultec/schematic/low-boost.ts` was left modified and unstaged**, and
  never staged.
- **The two `values` disagreements were not resolved on either side.** Which
  spelling the model should carry is not this guard's decision; they are recorded
  and asserted by name.
- **The four pre-existing `make check` failures were not fixed.** Fixing them
  would have hidden the comparison.
- **The findings in §5 were not acted on.** They are decisions about what the
  printed document should say.
- **The pre-existing test failures were not fixed**, and the flaky
  `tests/guide/print-pdf.test.ts` was not re-run until it agreed in either
  direction - it failed in the baseline and passed in the after run, and both
  readings are recorded rather than one of them being re-rolled.
- **`drafted/README.md` lost two figures rather than gaining corrected ones.** It
  said *"each of the four pages here"* and named `pultec-mid` as the board that
  declares nothing. Both are replaced with the greps that answer them, per
  CLAUDE.md: a figure in prose is a transcription with a date on it.
