# `SW_MID` pin 12: the eleventh board, and the seam the declaration went on

2026-10-29. What this run verified, what it built, what it measured on the page,
and the three things it deliberately did not do.

**The drawing in this document is UNREVIEWED.** This writer is not a qualified
reviewer of a drawing (`EVALUATING-DRAWINGS.md`), and nothing below is a verdict
on the page. The crops are in
[`2026-10-29-mid-pin12-nc/`](2026-10-29-mid-pin12-nc/) and exist to be shown to
the owner.

## What was wrong

`bun tools/schgen/board-sheets.ts` reported ten of eleven boards producing a
drawing, with `pultec-mid` `not-drawable`:

```
pultec-mid: not-drawable - NO DRAWING
    circuit is invalid:
      - part SW_MID: pin 12 (12) is neither on a net nor declared not connected,
        nor a mechanical mount point in lib/schgen/ir/mechanical-pins.ts
```

## The diagnosis, re-verified rather than taken on trust

**Read off `/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/SharedSupport/symbols/Switch.kicad_sym`,
KiCad 10.0.5.** `SW_Rotary_1x12` carries thirteen pins. Pins 1-12 are the throws
at x = +15.24, stepping 2.54 mm from y = +15.24 down to y = -15.24 with **no pin
at y = 0**; pin 13 is the pole at (-15.24, 0). Every pin is `passive` and every
pin's name is its own number. There is no `MP` pin and no `_MP` variant of this
symbol in the library at all.

The repository's own files agree, and said so before this task began.
`circuits/pultec/physical/parts.ts` on `ELEVEN_THROW_SYMBOL`: *"THE SYMBOL
THEREFORE CARRIES ONE MORE THROW THAN THE CIRCUIT USES ... twelve detents exist,
eleven are wired, the twelfth is unused"*, recording the owner's part choice
verbatim - ***"Assume a 1x12"***. `MID_POSITIONS` in
`circuits/pultec/electrical/mid.ts` has eleven entries, 200 Hz to 7 kHz, and
`switchOverride` in `circuits/pultec/schematic/section-board.ts` fills symbol
pins 1-11 and 13. **Pin 12 was the one pin nothing said anything about.**

## Where the declaration went, and why there

**`ComponentOverride.ncPins`** in `lib/schgen/authoring.ts`: a KiCad pin number
mapped to why *that* pin carries no connection.
`applyDeclaredNc` in `lib/schgen/from-network.ts` turns each entry into the IR's
ordinary `{ nc: true }` connection - the same thing a model pin tagged `NC`
produces - so the pin reaches `pinsDeclared` **declared**, and
`lib/schgen/ir/validate.ts` is untouched by the whole change.

The seam is the authoring layer because **the twelfth detent is a property of the
symbol chosen to draw the part, not of the circuit.** It sits beside
`symbolPins`, which already states which symbol pin carries which model pin, and
carries a mandatory `why` for the same reason that one does.

### The three wrong fixes, each ruled out before it was coded

| not this | why |
| --- | --- |
| `MECHANICAL_PINS` | That table's one entry is a panel mounting lug *no conductor inside the symbol reaches*, asserted geometrically against the installed library. Detent 12 is a `passive` throw at the same 2.54 mm pitch on the same side as the eleven that are wired. An entry there would exempt a genuinely electrical pin, which is the single defect `pinsDeclared` exists to catch, and which that file's own header warns about at length. |
| a twelfth throw in `circuits/pultec/electrical/` | For the Pultec the schematic is authoritative and the model is derived from it (`CLAUDE.md`, *"Authority runs in both directions"*). Electrically the mid section has eleven positions. A fictional twelfth throw would be a part-choice artifact in the electrical model. |
| `PartSpec.ncUnlisted` | The blanket - *"every symbol pin not declared in `pins` is a no-connect"*, used for an MCU's unused GPIOs. It would also excuse any pin a future KiCad adds to this symbol and any throw a later edit drops from the pin map. *"An empty whitelist that refuses loudly beats a general rule that quietly accepts a guess."* |

### `THROW_PINS`: a deviation from the proposed shape, recorded

The task proposed `ncPins` alone. This run also added `THROW_PINS` to
`section-board.ts`, beside the existing `POLE_PIN`, listing each selector
symbol's throw pins in detent order.

**It replaces an assumption that was already written down as a comment.**
`switchOverride` numbered the throws `String(index + 1)`. That is correct for
both rotary symbols this circuit uses and **nothing checked it**. Worse, without
it the unused-detent declaration would have needed a second statement of the same
library fact - the symbol's throw count - which is the second source of truth
this repository refuses. So the list drives both halves: the pin map indexes it,
and the detents past the circuit's last position are what is declared no-connect.

What it buys is the arm below that nothing else could make:

> **`THROW_PINS` plus `POLE_PIN` plus `MECHANICAL_PINS` is EXACTLY each selector
> symbol's pin set**, asserted against the installed library.

A pin added, removed or renumbered upstream fails that test by name, rather than
quietly becoming an unaccounted pin that stops a board drawing - or one a
blanket silently excused.

## The guards, and that each of them can fail

`tests/schgen/nc-pins.test.ts`, 20 arms. The ones that matter are the controls.

| the guard | where | the arm that proves it fires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pinsDeclared` still refuses an undeclared pin | unchanged | **strip the `ncPins` declaration from `PULTEC_MID_SCHEMATIC` and the original refusal comes back word for word** |
| it is specific, not a blanket | by construction | with `5` declared on the fixture part, pins `6` and `7` are still refused by name; with `5` and `7` declared, `6` still is |
| a declaration naming a pin the symbol does not have | `applyDeclaredNc` | an `ncPins` entry for pin `9` of an eight-pin symbol refuses, listing the symbol's real pins |
| a declared-NC pin that is ALSO on a net | `applyDeclaredNc` | refuses naming the pin and the net |
| a blank per-pin reason | `authoringProblems` | refuses, saying what the reason is for |
| the symbol's pin identity | installed library | pin 12 is `passive`, on the same side and at the same step as pin 11, and `mechanicalPinsOf("Switch:SW_Rotary_1x12").size` is `0` |

**THE STALE-ENTRY CHECK ITERATES THE DECLARATION AND NOT THE SYMBOL'S PINS**, and
that is the shape the task named: a stale entry names a pin the symbol does not
have, so a loop over the symbol's pins never reaches it and the guard could never
fire. `pinsDeclared`'s own `MECHANICAL_PINS` guard records the same lesson.

**`pinSemantics` could not have caught the net clash.** It refuses a library pin
*typed* `no_connect` that is on a net (`validate-nets.ts:47`). Every pin of
`SW_Rotary_1x12` is `passive`, so without the guard in `applyDeclaredNc` a pin
declared dead while a net ran to it would have drawn a no-connect marker on a
live wire with nothing reporting it.

## What the run reports now

```
11 of 11 boards produce a drawing: 10 usable, 1 defective
0 have no authoring declaration (G247)
```

**`pultec-mid` draws, and is classified `defective` on one finding that has
nothing to do with pin 12:**

```
[missing-junction] block pultec-mid-own: net mid_tap_2h branches at (26.67, 93.98)
(t-junction) and no junction is emitted there; KiCad joins a branch point only
with a junction object, so the drawing would show a connection the netlist does
not make
```

That is a generator defect this board has presumably always had, and **this is
the first run in which anybody could see it** - which is `G303` exactly: *"a gate
halts rendering and we never look at the picture so we can't tell if the gate is
insane or not"*. It is not fixed here and it is not this task's subject; it is
recorded so the next reader does not mistake it for a consequence of the
declaration.

  `UNRATIFIED - DEFECTIVE: this drawing is WRONG ABOUT THE CIRCUIT - DO NOT BUILD`.
  The sheet carries 203 wire segments, 28 junctions, 14 global labels and 10 text
  items.
- **The selector**: [`crop-selector-600dpi.png`](2026-10-29-mid-pin12-nc/crop-selector-600dpi.png).
  `SW1` is placed at (91.44, 83.82) rotated 180, so the throws run **12 at the

- **The marker**: [`crop-pin12-zoom-4x.png`](2026-10-29-mid-pin12-nc/crop-pin12-zoom-4x.png).

**WHERE PIN 12'S NO-CONNECT MARKER LANDS, measured rather than described.**

- The sheet carries **exactly one** `(no_connect ...)` item, at `(76.2, 68.58)`.
  That is pin 12's own connection point: the symbol origin `(91.44, 83.82)` less
  the library pin's `(15.24, 15.24)` under the 180° placement.
- Its ink is **1.355 mm across** on the 600 dpi render (31 px).
- **Nearest wire: 2.540 mm**, exactly one grid pitch, and there are three at that
  distance - the horizontal run at y = 66.04 above it, the vertical at x = 73.66
  to its left, and pin 11's own 2.54 mm stub below it. Taking the X's half-width
  off, **about 1.86 mm of clear space**.
- Nearest junction 9.67 mm, nearest global label 47.06 mm, nearest text item
  78.17 mm.
- **It does not overlap the pin-number glyphs.** Scanned row by row on the 600 dpi
  render, the X's rightmost blue pixel is strictly left of the nearest red pixel
  on every row; the closest approach is **5 px = 0.21 mm**, at the rows where the
  X's upper-right arm passes the bottom of the `12`. The only red ink the X
  crosses is **pin 12's own pin line**, which is where KiCad draws a no-connect
  marker by design.

**Observations, not a verdict.** The marker is drawn, it is at the pin, it is the
only one on the sheet, and it touches nothing but the pin it marks. 0.21 mm is
the tightest figure here and it is a gap rather than a collision. **Whether the
page is worth building from, or worth reading, is the owner's call and not this
writer's.**

## What the checks said

- `bun run typecheck`: clean.
- `bun test`, the whole suite: the only failures are **nine pre-existing
  environmental ones**, identical before and after the change. Eight are
  `tests/schgen/port-fidelity.test.ts` refusing because `SCHGEN_SOURCE` is not
  set (the `digital-pedal-platform` sources are not on this machine) - **4 pass /
  8 fail at `HEAD` in a clean worktree and 4 pass / 8 fail after**. The ninth is
  `tests/schgen/decomposition-probe.test.ts` timing out at bun's 5 s default -
  **5107 ms at `HEAD`, 9184 ms after**, so the change made a test that already
  failed slower, by letting `pultec-mid` build further than it used to.
  `tests/guide/print-pdf.test.ts` timed out once while a census was running
  alongside it and passes on its own.
- `make check`: **already failing before this change** and failing afterwards for
  the same four reasons, none of them in `lib/schgen/`. `typecheck` passes; four
  board checks under `boards/` fail - `pt2399-core` and the three
  `transistor-preamp` boards - on a missing `bomConditions` export and a footprint
  with no entry in `LEAD_SPANS`. Both runs are in the task report.

## What this leaves open

- **The `missing-junction` finding on `mid_tap_2h`** is unexamined. It is a
  generator question, it was invisible while the board refused, and it is the
  reason `pultec-mid` is `defective` rather than `usable`.
- **`pultec-mid` carries 12 fallback field placements**, the most of any board.
  Nothing here looked at what they cover.
- **No other circuit uses `ncPins`.** The mechanism has exactly one production
  caller, which is the shape `G226` asks to be declared rather than discovered:
  the precondition for a second is another circuit drawn with a symbol carrying
  more pins than it wires, and the price is that one board and one test file are
  the whole of its exercise.
