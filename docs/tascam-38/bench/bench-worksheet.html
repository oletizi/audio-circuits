Before you start Continuity checks are with the machine unpowered (mains disconnected). Card checks are on a card removed from the motherboard. The bias / erase circuit (J108-2, the U103 output) carries tens of volts AC at 150 kHz while recording - do not probe it with the record circuit live. Confirm pin 1 on each physical connector from the board silkscreen before measuring: the schematic numbers J105 / P105 from the opposite end to the other card connectors. Subtract the shorted-lead reading from low-ohm results.
