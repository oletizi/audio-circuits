# Tascam 38 electrical trace audit

Source: high-resolution amplifier schematic p2(1).pdf and owner's Tascam 38.xlsx Junctions worksheet.

The eight amplifier connectors have 50 contacts in total: J101 4, J102 7, J103 6, J104 7, J105 7, J106 8, J107 7, J108 4.

J101 card-side observations: pin 1 return, pin 2 K101 coil/D101 suppression circuit, pin 3 return, pin 4 reproduce signal contact feeding TR101. These observations do not independently verify motherboard-side routes.

Outstanding: verify head signal and erase routes, power and mute controls, and J102-5 versus J105-1/J105-2 conflict. Do not treat worksheet hypotheses as verified nets. The motherboard remains unchanged.
