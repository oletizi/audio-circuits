# J102/J105 cross-connections

Source: high-resolution amplifier sheet p2(1).pdf, right-hand connector area.

Card-side trace: J102 pin 5 joins J105 pin 2.

Motherboard trace: P102 pin 5 joins P105 pin 1.

These are distinct wires on different boards. This resolves the apparent disagreement in the owner's worksheet: one entry describes a card trace, the other describes a motherboard trace. Do not conflate card-side and motherboard-side endpoints when constructing the netlist.

The downstream audio/meter functions of these two wires still require tracing.
