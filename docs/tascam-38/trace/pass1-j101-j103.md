A note on the voltage labels. The labels "24V" and "-15V" on these nets are
drawn in the same italic hand as the DC-voltage annotations used throughout the
sheet (e.g. "20.6V", "-5.8V", "0.6V", "23.4V", "-6.8V"). They are recorded below
as printed, but they may be measured node voltages rather than rail names. The
drawing labels BOTH the J103-4 net and the J103-5 net "-15V", with R127 100k
between them - so at least one of those two labels is not a unique rail name.

Against the hypotheses
----------------------
- J101: K101 and D101 are confirmed (J101-1/J101-2 = coil + flyback diode,
  J101-4 = a relay contact throw). TR101 is NOT directly on any J101 contact:
  TR101 pin 1 is reached only through the K101 contact (pole side), and TR101
  pins 2/4 sit on the unlabelled heavy bus that J101-3 feeds. No reproduce-head
  label appears on any J101 card-side wire; whether J101-4 carries the head
  signal is not shown on the card side.
- J103: the "-15V" label does appear (on J103-4 and on J103-5). No OUT SELECT,
  sync or erase label appears on any J103 card-side wire. What the drawing
  shows is J103-2 and J103-4 driving D104/D102 cathodes (toward Q103/Q101
  gates) with 100k resistors R129/R127 to the J103-5 line, and J103-6 feeding
  R126 82k. J103-1 and J103-3 have no card-side wire.
