METHOD NOTE
Besides visual crops (3-10x), two helper scripts were used, both in
scratchpad/agent2-p106-p108/:
- tracer.py walks a wire pixel by pixel from a start point, follows corners,
  and lists every perpendicular line it meets with a "flank" thickness (how
  thick the walked line is just beside the crossing).
- dotprobe.py prints dark-run heights in columns either side of a crossing.
Calibration: the dot at (5345,2608) on the card (Pass 1 known dot) and the
known no-dot crossings at (5370,2608)/(5395,2608). A real dot shows flanks
tapering 8-15 px over ~5 px either side of the crossing line; a plain crossing
shows flanks equal to the line's normal thickness (2-3 px thin, 5-7 px thick).
Every dot claimed below was confirmed both numerically and in a crop; every
"no dot" at a place that mattered was confirmed in a crop as well. Printed
connector numbers were read from crops, not inferred.

Recurring scan artifacts (not wiring): a vertical page seam at x ~4397-4402
and another at x ~2303, each breaking every horizontal by 1-4 px; horizontals
are collinear across both seams. A scan-damage patch at x 5390-5440,
y 3045-3070 (see P106-2).
