# Digital pedal platform, phase 1: porting the design knowledge from the pedals repository

**Status:** approved by the owner, 2026-11-10 (*"approved"*), as revised in
response to the third-party review in issue #27. Next: the implementation
plan. Branch: `feature/digital-pedal-platform`.

## Intent

The owner is consolidating all of the project's electronics work into this
repository and will archive `oletizi/pedals` (archived, not deleted - it stays
readable). The first thing to come across is the **STM32H743 digital pedal
platform** ("h7-platform"), developed on the pedals branch
`feature/digital-pedal-platform`.

The reason for consolidating is the tooling. In the owner's words:

> *"I want to use ONLY the tooling in this repo--the newer
> copperhead->akcli->kicad-cli loop. That's the primary reason why I want to
> consolidate electronics discipline into this repo--the tooling we arrived at
> was hard won. I do NOT want to poison it with old garbage from the pedals
> repo"*

What the owner wants from this repository for the platform:

> *"I want to be able to develop the circuits, schematics, and pcbs and be able
> to send them to production at pcbway or similar from this repo"*

The platform is developed on this branch until a prototype PCB is ready to send
to PCBWay. That end point is far off and is not given a date (*"that's a long
way off--false precision in planning"*).

## The phases, and what this document covers

The work runs in phases. Each later phase gets its own design when its turn
comes, because each depends on what the earlier ones find.

1. **The port (this document).** The design knowledge comes across into this
   repository's structure. Nothing is modelled or drawn.
2. **The models.** Each board is re-authored as a `Network` in `circuits/`.
3. **The schematics.** Each board is drawn by `TOOLING.md`'s loop and shown to
   the owner under `EVALUATING-DRAWINGS.md`.
4. **Layout and the fabrication package.** PCB layout, and what PCBWay needs for
   fabrication and assembly, including the fully specified BOM that only binds
   here (CLAUDE.md, G245).

The pedals design names three boards: the core processor module (h7-module),
the audio pedal board (h7-pedal), and a minimal bring-up carrier (O-13). The
order they go through phases 2-4 is for phase 2's design, not this one.

## What is in the pedals repository

The source is `git@github.com:oletizi/pedals.git`, branch
`feature/digital-pedal-platform`, **pinned at commit
`5b926d0aaf002d3ea111786f6ab9cdb72b8a913e`**. Every citation this port writes
uses a GitHub link at that commit, so it keeps resolving after the repository is
archived.

The design history in brief:

- **A1/A2 (2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04):** one monolithic board, `boards/v1`. It
  has six sheets: core (MCU, SDRAM, QSPI), codec (PCM3060), audio I/O, power,
  controls and USB/debug. It was generated by the pedals repository's own
  schematic generator (`tools/schgen`) from `h7-platform/circuit/*.ts`.
- **The modular split (2026-10-05, owner-approved):**
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-h7-modular-split-design.md`. A socketed
  processor module on the Daisy Seed 40-pin pinout plugs into an audio pedal
  board, in a Hammond 1590BB. Owner decisions O-1 to O-18 and controller
  rulings RR-1 to RR-11 are in its §2 and §2.1.
- **Split stage S1 (the module contract), done through its Task 17 follow-ups.**
  It produced the draft contract `h7-platform/module-interface.json`, the
  electrical analyses in `h7-platform/interface-analysis/`, the envelope and
  controls studies, and the parts the owner chose (footswitch, soft buttons,
  module socket, enclosure).
- **Split stage S2 (splitting the schematic): planned, never started.** No
  layout exists. The contract is still `"status": "draft"`: it was never frozen
  (split spec §13 step 4).

## Goals

1. **Every live owner decision about the platform is recorded in this
   repository**, verbatim where his words exist, with a link to where it was
   made. "Live" means it still binds the split design: O-1 to O-18 as amended,
   the D-numbered CORE-BRIEF deltas he approved, the OWN-xx defaults that still
   stand, and the owner decisions in the S1 rulings ledger
   (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-h7-split-rulings.md`).
   **An entry counts as an owner decision only if the ledger itself says so**:
   it is titled "Owner decision" (R-34, R-43) or quotes his answer under
   "Owner decision, quoted" (R-56). Everything else in the ledger is a
   controller ruling, and the port labels it that way, as something he may
   override. That includes R-55, R-60, R-61, and R-62, which is a controller
   ruling on **his measurements** of the enclosure. Measurements he took
   are carried as facts he supplied, separately from the ruling built on them.
   An entry that says "confirmed" without saying who confirmed it is carried
   as a controller ruling, with that ambiguity noted beside it.
   *(Corrected after the builder check. The first draft called R-55 to R-62
   owner rulings; the ledger labels them controller rulings.)*
2. **The design content the next phases need is here, rewritten in this
   repository's vocabulary**, as documentation under `docs/h7-platform/`:
   - the split design: architecture, the two boards' contents, the interface
     rules and the bring-up carrier's scope;
   - the parts already chosen, with MPN, LCSC code where one was chosen, and
     the source each choice rests on;
   - the analyses the circuit rests on and their results: power, analog, bypass
     relay, SDRAM compatibility, and the S1 interface analyses (VREF_A, VIN_MOD,
     3V3_D, codec domain, crossings, power-fail, pedal digital rail, envelope,
     controls);
   - the layout carry-forward: what layout must honour from the schematic
     (`LAYOUT-CARRYFORWARD.md`), restated against the split boards;
   - the schematic checklist content that is about the circuit rather than about
     the old generator (`SCHEMATIC-CHECKLIST.md`);
   - the open risks and unverified facts (split spec §14, §16), each still
     marked as open;
   - **a carry-forward table of every unresolved requirement, allowance and
     freeze blocker** (issue #27, finding 1). It covers split spec §14 and §16.
     It also covers every "Not verified" and "Carries" section of each S1
     analysis and every later ruling. Each row names:
     - the affected board and power domain;
     - the current status;
     - the evidence that would close it;
     - the phase that must close it;
     - the pinned source.

     It must include at least VDDA-WIDEBAND, VBUS-HOTPLUG and FB-AN2606 from
     R-57. Every figure in it is a **paper analysis of a board that has not
     been built**. The table says so, and says nothing that reads as a hardware
     failure or as a closure;
   - **hardware requirements that firmware imposes or depends on** (issue #27,
     finding 3), restated against the split architecture:
     - the Seed H750/QSPI versus H743/internal-flash configurations, and 32 MB
       SDRAM compatibility;
     - the bootloader/application flash map and its recovery limits;
     - codec-master SAI2, pin states that depend on power domain, relay and
       watchdog sequencing, and USB update enumeration.

     They are recorded as constraints on the hardware. This repository does not
     own firmware, and the section does not set it up to;
   - **the bring-up acceptance checklist**: `SCHEMATIC-CHECKLIST.md` §9,
     BU-01 to BU-29, and any BL items. Original source identifiers are kept for
     traceability. Each item is marked as an original requirement, a deferred
     measurement or a paper analysis that passed.
3. **The module contract is here as data**, at
   `circuits/h7-platform/module-interface.json`, still marked draft. It is a
   source that will be edited here, because the analyses that fill in and
   freeze it are not finished. So it sits with the circuit and not under
   `docs/` (CLAUDE.md, "One directory per circuit").
   - **The analysis results file crosses with it, also unchanged:**
     `interface-analysis/results.json`, with 76 values, each carrying its source,
     goes to `circuits/h7-platform/interface-analysis-results.json`. It is a
     source still being edited, for the same reason as the contract. The prose
     analyses (Goal 2) are what explain it.
   - **The contract's citations stay as they are.** Its `source` fields cite pedals
     paths and sections ("interface-analysis/envelope.md §3", "spec §5.6.3",
     R-61, D-A-1). Rewriting them is not part of this phase. Instead, the README
     carries a **provenance index** (issue #27, finding 4). It maps each alias
     used in either JSON file to an exact pinned source file:
     - document aliases (`spec`, `PW`, `AN`, `CB` and the like), saying which of
       the two platform specs an unqualified `spec` means;
     - the prefixes of ruling and review identifiers (R-, RR-, D-A- and so on);
     - relative path roots such as `vendor/st-pin-data/...`.

     The index says these strings are historical provenance, not paths that
     resolve here. The index carries three worked traces, each to its source: a
     rail limit, an S1 amendment, and a pin-data path. Each ported document
     names the pedals file and sections it came from. Re-pointing the citations
     is a contract edit, so it belongs to whichever later phase next edits the
     contract.
4. **A reader can tell what came across and what did not.** A README at
   `docs/h7-platform/README.md` says what the platform is, where each part of
   the knowledge now lives, what was deliberately left in the pedals
   repository, and how to reach it.
   - **The mapping covers the platform's files, not the whole pedals
     repository.** At the pinned commit that means:
     - everything under `h7-platform/`;
     - the platform's specs (`2026-09-30-h7-pedal-platform-design.md`, which
       the split spec amends section by section, and
       `2026-10-05-h7-modular-split-design.md`);
     - four documents outside that directory that carry platform knowledge:
       `docs/research/2026-09-30-proven-reference-designs.md`,
       `docs/research/2026-10-05-processor-module-form-factors.md`,
       `docs/standards/pcbway-fab-rules.md` and the S1 rulings ledger.

     Whole directories that stay behind (`boards/v1/`, `mechanical/`,
     `interface-analysis/sources/` and so on) can each be one row. The pedals
     repository's other projects (fuzz-v2, perfboard and so on) belong to their
     own ports.
   - **The two research documents and the PCBWay fabrication rules cross**, to
     `docs/h7-platform/research/` and `docs/standards/pcbway-fab-rules.md`. The
     fabrication rules are not specific to this platform, but phase 4 needs
     them, and this repository has no `docs/standards/` yet.
5. **The OSCAR licences and attributions come across.** `h7-platform/LICENSE-OSCAR`
   records that the core MCU, SDRAM and flash circuitry derives from DADDesign
   OSCAR_PROC_01 (MIT). The codec circuitry derives from OSCAR_AUDIO_01, for
   which the attribution file records Apache-2.0 alongside MIT. It records the
   discrepancy between the two upstream statements as **unresolved**. Both files
   go to `circuits/h7-platform/` unchanged. The README says which circuitry they
   cover and repeats that the OSCAR_AUDIO_01 licence statement is unresolved
   upstream. It must not paraphrase it as settled. That obligation
   follows the circuit into the phase-2 models and the phase-4 fabrication
   files, and it costs nothing to carry now. Finding it only at fabrication
   would cost more.
6. **The picture of the chosen control layout comes across as a record**:
   `interface-analysis/layouts/layout-B.svg`, the layout the owner chose ("I
   prefer B", O-18), goes beside the decisions document. A pedals script
   generated it, but it is carried as the picture he chose from, not as
   output to regenerate. Nothing here rebuilds it.

## Non-goals

- **No code from the pedals repository**, of any kind: not the generator, the
  gates, the pin-map tools, the interface checker, the analysis calculators, or
  their tests. If the platform needs a check, phases 2-4 write it fresh against
  this repository's model.
- **No circuit model and no drawing.** Those are phases 2 and 3.
- **No generated artifacts from the old tooling:** `boards/v1/*` (schematics,
  PDF, locks, REFMAP, render reports), `generated/`, `pinmap.json`,
  `PINMAP.md`.
- **No pedals-specific KiCad library files:** `symbols/H7.kicad_sym`,
  `footprints/H7.pretty`, `pintables/`. When a later phase needs a custom symbol
  or footprint, it remakes it and checks it against the installed KiCad
  libraries (CLAUDE.md, "Verify names against the thing itself"). The pedals
  copies can be consulted by link.
- **No mechanical files yet:** the STEP models and their generators
  (`h7-platform/mechanical/`, 47 MB) stay put until layout needs them. The
  mechanical *decisions* (enclosure, stack height, envelope, control positions)
  are knowledge and come across under Goal 2.
- **No datasheet copies:** `interface-analysis/sources/` holds 80 MB of vendor
  PDFs. They are cited by link, or by the vendor's URL where the pedals
  repository records one.
- **No process record:** plans, rulings ledgers, review rounds, `VERIFICATION.md`
  and `DESIGN-REVIEW.md` results stay behind. The owner: *"You can always refer
  back to the pedals repo. It will not get deleted, just archived."*
- **Nothing in the pedals repository is changed** by this phase.

## Anti-goals: what would make this a failure even if every file landed

- **Pedals tooling arriving under another name.** For example, a calculator
  re-typed as a "helper", a gate re-typed as a test, or the schgen IR shape
  (`PartSpec`, `BlockSpec`, `sheetKit`) used to describe parts in the ported
  documents. The documents describe the circuit in plain engineering terms and
  this repository's vocabulary (semantic ids, kind pin names), not in the old
  generator's types.
- **The v1 schematic treated as the authority.** It came from tooling the owner
  has rejected, and the split superseded it. The authorities are the owner's
  decisions, the datasheets and the analyses. Where the ported text quotes what
  v1 contained, it says that is all it is.
- **Stale or refuted content carried as current.** POWER.md and ANALOG.md use
  pre-split names, and the split spec §9 amends them. The port applies those
  amendments, or marks clearly what is pre-split, rather than copying the
  stale text.
- **A figure stripped of its basis.** CLAUDE.md: *"a figure in prose is a
  transcription with a date on it."* Each analysis result crosses with its
  inputs, its method in words and its source, so phase 2 can re-derive it
  against the model. A bare number does not count as ported.
- **A finding of this phase recorded in `docs/known-gaps.md`.** That register is
  frozen except for owner decisions. Undiagnosed observations go in this spec's
  "What this leaves open" section, or in the phase-2 design.

## Constraints

- `CONSTITUTION.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `EVALUATING-DRAWINGS.md` bind.
- **Transcription is evidence, not memory.** Every ported fact is read from the
  pedals file at the pinned commit, and the ported document says which file it
  came from.
- **The model will be authoritative** for this circuit when it exists (it is
  agent-designed, not an imported schematic). These documents are its inputs.
- **Owner decisions** go in `docs/known-gaps.md` *only* if a new one is made
  during this phase. Ported historical decisions live in `docs/h7-platform/`
  with their pedals provenance. They are not new register entries.
- **Commit and push** each coherent piece as it lands, on
  `feature/digital-pedal-platform`.

## How amendments resolve (issue #27, finding 2)

The pedals sources were amended many times after the split spec. The split spec
§9, the S1 analyses, R-1 onward, and the Task 17 rulings all amend earlier
text. "Carry both versions and mark one possibly superseded" leaves phase 2 with
no baseline, so the port resolves amendments by rule:

1. **Authority first, then date.**
   - An owner decision is superseded only by a later owner decision.
   - Among controller rulings, and among analyses, the later dated text
     supersedes the earlier text it names or amends.
   - **A controller ruling never supersedes an owner decision.** A ruling that
     narrows one is carried as a narrowing that the owner may override, and it
     is linked to the decision it narrows.
   - Note that O-15, despite its O-number, is recorded in the split spec as a
     controller ruling. That makes R-50's 9 V Seed bench rule a ruling
     narrowing a ruling, not a ruling overriding the owner.
2. **The effective constraint is stated once, as current**, with a link to each
   source that set or amended it. Superseded values appear only in a history
   line marked as not in force.
3. **Where the sources cannot settle it**, for example two rulings with no
   stated ordering, or an analysis that contradicts a ruling without amending
   it, the port writes an explicit **conflict entry** naming both and does not
   pick one. A conflict that changes what gets built is put to the owner.
4. Where it isn't obvious whether a passage is still live, carry it and mark
   it, with the reason. This is the residual case, not the default.
- How to represent each S1 analysis result so that its inputs and method travel
  with the number.

## Done when

- Every file named under Goal 2 exists, and the README (Goal 4) maps each
  pedals source file at the pinned commit to where its content went, or to
  "stayed behind" with the reason.
- `circuits/h7-platform/module-interface.json`,
  `interface-analysis-results.json`, `LICENSE-OSCAR` and `LICENSE-APACHE-2.0`
  are byte-identical to the pedals files at the pinned commit (compare the
  sha256 of each). Any change to them is a later phase's work, made in a commit
  of its own.
- Owner decisions O-1 to O-18 and every ledger entry the ledger itself marks
  as an owner decision (Goal 1) appear in the decisions document, each with its
  link. Controller rulings that still bind appear separately, labelled as
  rulings.
- No file under the paths this phase adds contains TypeScript or a script, or
  names a pedals tool as something to run.
- `docs/h7-platform/README.md` maps every platform file listed under Goal 4,
  and its provenance index resolves the three worked traces (Goal 3).
- The carry-forward table contains VDDA-WIDEBAND, VBUS-HOTPLUG and FB-AN2606,
  each still open and each naming the phase that must close it.
- The effective constraints state, as current and with their supersession
  links:
  - the 9 V Seed bench restriction (R-50, R-59);
  - the 421 Hz control-noise band (R-57);
  - the Q_PEN reset of 3V3_P's EN (R-58).

  In **newly written prose**, none of the superseded values (9-12 V, 179 Hz,
  the diode reset) appears except in a history line marked as not in force.
  The byte-identical records (the two JSON files, the licences and the
  layout-B picture) are exempt and are not edited. They still say, for
  example, "Seed benches run on 9–12 V supplies only (O-15)". The README
  warns that these records contain superseded statements, names the
  superseded values, and links each one to its effective constraint.
  *(Clarified after the issue #27 follow-up review; the approved intent is
  unchanged.)* *These three are a sample
  that a reviewer can check, not the whole of the requirement.*
- The firmware-facing requirements and the bring-up checklist exist, and the
  bring-up checklist has BU-01 to BU-29 by their original identifiers.
- `bun run typecheck` and `make check` are unaffected (nothing here is code; run
  them anyway to show it).

## What this leaves open

- Where the platform's firmware lives. This repository's mandate names five
  hardware output classes and no firmware. Phase 1 records the hardware side of
  the firmware contract only.

- The order in which the three boards go through modelling and drawing (phase
  2's design).
- How the contract's remaining analyses are completed and the contract frozen,
  and whether a check in this repository then validates the contract against
  the model (phase 2).
- The module's internal MCU pin assignments (SDRAM, QSPI, crystal and so on),
  which the old pin map and v1 core sheet carried and the contract does not.
  Phase 2 re-derives them from the datasheet and ST's pin data, citing the
  pedals record.
