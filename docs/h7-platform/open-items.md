## Conflicts

### Conflict: the Rev4 Daisy Seed's codec and how a Rev4 is detected

- **One side:** Electrosmith's Daisy Seed datasheet `DS120` v1.2.0, p. 13 ("Hardware Revisions"), names Rev4's codec only through Rev5's reason for change, "AKM factory fire, AK4556 discontinued", so Rev4 carries an AK4556. It also says "Rev4/Rev7 do not use I2C pins. Auto-software detects hardware as Rev 4 Daisy in libDaisy", while its own table gives Rev7 a version pin (PD5).
- **The other side:** libDaisy (`src/daisy_seed.h`, commit `08087203`) describes Rev5 as using "the WM8731 codec instead of the AK4430", so Rev4 carries an AK4430; and `DaisySeed::CheckBoardVersion()` detects Rev7 by PD5 low, treating only a board with none of PD3, PD4, PD5 and PH6 low as Rev4.
- **Why neither is picked:** both are Electrosmith's own sources, neither amends the other, and no dated statement settles them ([facts.md §8](https://github.com/oletizi/pedals/blob/5b926d0aaf002d3ea111786f6ab9cdb72b8a913e/h7-platform/interface-analysis/facts.md) records the disagreement and leaves it).
- **What it affects:** only the identification of a Rev4 at first bring-up (open item SEED-REVISION-RECORD; [split spec §14 R-2](https://github.com/oletizi/pedals/blob/5b926d0aaf002d3ea111786f6ab9cdb72b8a913e/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-h7-modular-split-design.md) uses the codec marking as the authority and names the AK4556). A Rev4 is not used until its VIN range is confirmed from an Electrosmith document, so the conflict changes nothing that gets built. Settled by an Electrosmith statement of the Rev4's codec, or by reading the codec marking on a Rev4 in hand.

### Conflict: the v1 test pads on the split boards

- **One side:** `SCHEMATIC-CHECKLIST.md` PWR-16 and USB-07, and `LAYOUT-CARRYFORWARD.md` "Placement and routing" (test pads), for the one v1 board: one pogo grid on one side (SWDIO, SWCLK, NRST, BOOT0, GND, 3V3_D, 5V_A, VIN_PROT, and VIN before the Schottky), bench pads off the grid on VDDA, VCAP and VIN_BUCK, each with a GND pad, and audio pads beside GND pads.
- **The other side:** split spec §9.3 ("PWR-16 split", "USB-07 split"), §6.1 (the SWD header "and its pogo pads", the debug subset, to the module) and §7.1 (the pedal "keeps the power and audio pads"); [D15](decisions.md#d15) keeps the VCAP and VDDA bench pads on the module.
- **Why neither is picked:** the split names the halves, not the pads. VIN_PROT no longer exists (VIN_A and VIN_MOD replace it), so which net the v1 VIN_PROT pad lands on is unstated; whether the pedal also carries 3V3_D and GND pogo pads, whether each board has a grid of its own, and where the VIN_BUCK bench pad goes now that BU-25 measures at the module input, no source says. Assigning them is a design choice, not a reading of the sources.
- **What it affects:** test access only: the pogo fixture and the bring-up measurements' pads (BU-25, the hot plug at the module input; BU-03, the 3.3 V minimum at the MCU). It does not change the circuit. Settled by phase 2's per-board pad lists (open item TEST-PAD-SPLIT).
