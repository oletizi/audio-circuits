# h7-platform bring-up checklist

Ported from:
- [h7-platform/SCHEMATIC-CHECKLIST.md][chk] §9, "Handed to bring-up and firmware (not schematic items)": BU-01 to BU-29, by their original identifiers
- [2026-10-05-h7-modular-split-design.md][split] (the split spec): §6.4 (BL-1 to BL-4, the PB15 and PB14 ROM-boot cautions), §9.3 (the BU rework and the added checks), §12.4 (the bring-up order), §13 step 1 (as withdrawn by O-16), §13.1 (the carrier's acceptance)
- [h7-platform/LAYOUT-CARRYFORWARD.md][lcf]: "Hand checks before and at first bring-up" and the first-build checks of its two split sections
- [h7-platform/BYPASS-RELAY.md][br] §9, which added BU-27 to BU-29 and replaced BU-08, BU-11 and BU-19

This is the acceptance checklist for the first boards of the STM32H743 digital pedal platform (h7-platform) after the split into a processor module (**h7-module**), an audio pedal board (**h7-pedal**) and the bring-up carrier ([O-13](decisions.md#o-13), owner). `SCHEMATIC-CHECKLIST.md` handed these items to bring-up and firmware for the one-board v1; split spec §9.3 reworks six of them and adds others. Each item keeps its original identifier, carries its current meaning, and links the topic section that holds its figures, which are not restated here. Where an item is the measurement that closes a row of [open-items.md](open-items.md), the row is named. It records the design as it stood in the pedals repository at commit `5b926d0`; nothing has been built.

**Every figure this checklist links is paper analysis of a board that has not been built.** No item here is passed: each stays open until its measurement or check is made on a built board.

**This repository does not own firmware.** Several items are things firmware must do; they are carried as the hardware's reliance on firmware, as [firmware-facing-requirements.md](firmware-facing-requirements.md) carries them.

Decisions are linked by anchor into [decisions.md](decisions.md), whose labels this document follows. "The carrier" means the bring-up carrier of [O-13](decisions.md#o-13) only; the S1 analyses' "carrier" is h7-pedal.

## 1. The three markings

Each BU line carries exactly one marking, giving its status at the pinned commit. **The marking is this document's reading of each item, not a source's classification**; the reason is given where it is not plain from the item.

- *original requirement*: a requirement the checklist states as it stood, usually something firmware or the bring-up procedure must do, with no analysis behind it that could pass or fail.
- *deferred measurement*: a quantity no paper analysis settled, left to a measurement on a built board.
- *passed paper analysis*: a check whose paper analysis exists and passed with its stated margin; the measurement confirms it on hardware.

## 2. The order of bring-up

Split spec §12.4, as amended. [architecture.md](architecture.md) §9 maps the order of work onto this repository's phases.

1. **Audio board with a real Daisy Seed fitted** ([O-9](decisions.md#o-9), owner). Current-limited first power, on a 9 V supply while a Seed is fitted ([power.md](power.md) [EC-SEED-BENCH](power.md#ec-seed-bench), which carries O-15 as narrowed by R-50 and its history line). Before any other check on that board, the three Seed facts no document settles, in the order R-2a, R-2b, R-2c ([O-16](decisions.md#o-16), owner; open items SEED-ONBOARD-LOADS, SEED-PIN38-PIN21-LOAD, SEED-BOOTLOADER-PIN-STATES), with the Seed's revision recorded first (open item SEED-REVISION-RECORD). Then the audio-board checks: codec master clocks, SAI lock, analog passband and level, relays and power-loss timing (BU-08, BU-19, BU-28 as reworked), controls, USB, MIDI, noise. Module-specific checks (the SDRAM sequence, the HSE, our supervisor) are not run here; the Seed is a stand-in, not the product. No 18 V test is run with a Seed fitted (EC-SEED-BENCH).
2. **Our module on the carrier** ([O-13](decisions.md#o-13)): first power current-limited, so a module fault cannot damage an audio board. Then SWD connect, the HSE (BU-05), the SDRAM test sequence (BU-07), flash, the ROM DFU on the module's own USB socket with the carrier's USB-C disconnected, the module identification strap, and the bootloader in flash (BL-1, BL-2: flash it, then confirm USB enumeration on pins 36/37 from the bootloader). The carrier's own acceptance: the module powers up current-limited without a fault, the SAI slave locks to the loopback clocks, and pins 36/37 enumerate from the bootloader ([architecture.md](architecture.md) §7).
3. **Our module in the working audio board.** The full BU list, including the PB15 ROM-boot check (the pedal's USB-C unplugged before BOOT0 entry), the USB switch checks and the cross-domain measurements (IF-PD-1) at power-up and power-down in both orders (§4).
4. **Parity**: the same firmware source, built for each board configuration, gives the same CDC check results on a Seed and on our module in the same audio board, apart from the board-identification and memory-size lines (open item SEED-MODULE-PARITY).

*Not in force:* split spec §12.4 step 0, a bare Seed3 characterised on the bench before any board of ours exists. The owner's [O-16](decisions.md#o-16) withdrew it (split spec §13 step 1); its three measurements move to the start of step 1.

## 5. BL-1 to BL-4

Split spec §6.4 sets these on the hardware and on the firmware contract ([O-11](decisions.md#o-11), owner). Their text is carried in [firmware-facing-requirements.md](firmware-facing-requirements.md) §3.1; what bring-up checks of each is below.

- **BL-1** · *original requirement* · h7-module · Room for our bootloader at the start of internal flash, the application linked above it. Neither the flash sector size nor the reserved size was ever set. Step 2 flashes the bootloader (split spec §12.4). Open item BOOTLOADER-FLASH-MAP.
- **BL-2** · *original requirement* · h7-module, the carrier and h7-pedal · USB on pins 36/37 enumerable from the bootloader, with nothing outside the 40-pin contract and without the application having run; the pedal's USB-C path must not stop a host from enumerating it. Checked at step 2 on the carrier and at step 3 in the pedal ([firmware-facing-requirements.md](firmware-facing-requirements.md) [EC-USB-OTG-HS](firmware-facing-requirements.md#ec-usb-otg-hs)). Open items RECOVERY-PATH-CHECKS and USB-SWITCH-FS-IMPEDANCE.
- **BL-3** · *original requirement* · h7-pedal with a Seed · On a Seed the Daisy bootloader stays the update path, through the Seed's own micro-USB; it does not drive pin 11, so the pedal's USB switch stays open while it runs ([R-14](decisions.md#r-14) amends BL-3; [architecture.md](architecture.md) §5, "BL-3 on a Seed"; [firmware-facing-requirements.md](firmware-facing-requirements.md) §3.2). The sources name no bring-up check for it.
- **BL-4** · *original requirement* · h7-module · The ROM DFU and SWD stay available however the bootloader is written or damaged: the readout-protection option bytes kept at a level that leaves both usable. Step 2 exercises SWD and the ROM DFU (split spec §12.4); no source names a check with a damaged bootloader. Open items BOOTLOADER-FLASH-MAP and RECOVERY-PATH-CHECKS.

## 6. First-build checks from the layout carry-forward

`LAYOUT-CARRYFORWARD.md` lists hand checks on top of the BU items. On the split ([layout-carry-forward.md](layout-carry-forward.md)):

- **The enclosure**: an ordered box against the owner-measured reference, the lip within 4 mm of the wall, the back plate seated with the jacks fitted ([R-62](decisions.md#r-62) on the owner's [F-5](decisions.md#f-5)). Open item ENCLOSURE-FIRST-BUILD.
- **The chassis bond**: enclosure-to-GND continuity after coating, the reading recorded ([PW-CHASSIS](decisions.md#pw-chassis), controller ruling). Open item ENCLOSURE-FIRST-BUILD.
- **The DC jack**: a standard 5.5 × 2.1 mm plug fits the fitted jack. Open item DC-JACK-FIT.
- **The footswitches**: on the first assembled footswitch of each build, the switch closes on a full press, is open at rest, and the spring is not coil-bound; one shim corrects a gap. Open item CONTROLS-FIRST-BUILD.
- **The module sockets**: all 40 contacts continuous after one insertion, and the module held. Open item MODULE-SOCKET-MATE.
- **The trimmed leads and socket tails** measured against their limits, each trimmed joint reflowed or inspected. Open item TRIM-RULES-ORDER.
- **The expression input's back-feed gate**: with the module unpowered and +5 V on the ring, VREF_A reads at most 0.6 V, at room temperature, at the hottest bench condition and through a slow 0 to +5 V sweep. **A failure means the unpowered blocking does not hold: stop and redesign the ring reference before use.** Open item VREF-A-BACKFEED-GATE.
- **VREF_A's noise at pin 21** with our module fitted: in the 0–421 Hz control band at most 1,123 µV rms, and wideband, to the ADC's sampling bandwidth, at most 806 µV rms ([R-15](decisions.md#r-15), [R-57](decisions.md#r-57); [analog.md](analog.md) [EC-CONTROL-NOISE-BAND](analog.md#ec-control-noise-band), [EC-VDDA-WIDEBAND](analog.md#ec-vdda-wideband)). Open item VREF-A-NOISE-MEASURE.
- **The module's DFU socket**: hold the module while pulling the plug. Open item USB-DFU-VERTICAL.
